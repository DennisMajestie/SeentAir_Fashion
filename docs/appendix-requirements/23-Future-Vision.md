# 23. Future Vision

## Explicitly NOT part of the initial platform direction

- Multiple factories.
- A marketplace connecting unrelated third-party sellers.
- An affiliate/commission model disguised as the partner system — the partner system is specifically for business partners/investors (see `17-Partners-Investors.md`).

## Longer-term direction (post-MVP)

- Multiple physical stores/locations.
- More distribution locations.
- A large online clothing store.
- Wider customer reach.
- More automation.
- More logistics integration.
- Future POS/mall system integration (full spec, beyond MVP connection points).
- More sophisticated sales and business analytics.

## The single most important outcome (guiding principle)

> "Customers can shop without struggling, while the owner can see what is happening in the business without needing to be physically present at the factory."

This should be treated as one of the main design principles guiding the whole product, and used as a north star when prioritizing between competing features.
