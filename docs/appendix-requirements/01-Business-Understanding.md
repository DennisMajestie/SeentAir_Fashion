# 01. Business Understanding

## Company

Seentair Limited — a fashion manufacturing factory operating from a single factory location.

## Core business

- Focus: streetwear and clothing.
- Serves children, teens, and adults.
- Sells to: the general public (retail), retailers, wholesalers, and distributors/bulk buyers.

## Strategic goals

- Grow beyond the current physical-only operation.
- Build a strong online clothing store.
- Increase sales and make purchasing easier for customers.
- Give the owner visibility into the business without needing to be physically present at the factory ("a heartbeat of the business").
- Give business partners/investors transparency into how their funds are performing — explicitly, the client wants partners to "not feel cheated in any way."

## Platform framing

This is **not** just an online store. It is a central business system connecting manufacturing, inventory, sales (retail + wholesale), e-commerce, accounting, logistics, analytics, and partner/investor visibility, all in one place.

## Confirmed by client

YES, this is an accurate description of the business (with the partner transparency note above added).
