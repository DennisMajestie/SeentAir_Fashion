# 15. Marketing & Sales Growth

## Business objective

Increasing sales is the client's major business objective, so the platform should help the business understand **where sales are coming from**, in addition to running marketing activity.

## Capabilities to support

- Marketing campaigns
- Promotions
- Sales-channel tracking (attribute each sale to its source/channel)
- Customer recommendations
- Loyalty programs
- Product visibility/boosting
- Sales performance analysis

## Data/entities implied

- `SaleChannel` (order id, channel/source)
- `Campaign` (name, type, start/end date, linked promotions)
