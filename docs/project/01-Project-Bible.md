# 01. Project Bible — Seentair Limited Platform

## What this document is

The single, always-current reference for what we are building and why. When in doubt, this is the tie-breaker. Detailed specs live in the other documents; this one holds the **intent**.

## Client

- **Business:** Seentair Limited
- **Contact:** Okereke Ndubuka Cynthia
- **Industry:** Fashion manufacturing (streetwear/clothing), one factory
- **Customers served:** general public (retail), retailers, wholesalers, distributors/bulk buyers — children, teens, and adults

## The one-sentence vision

A central business system connecting manufacturing, inventory, sales (retail + wholesale), e-commerce, accounting, logistics, analytics, and partner/investor visibility — replacing manual, paper-based operations with one connected digital platform.

## The guiding principle (north star)

> "Customers can shop without struggling, while the owner can see what is happening in the business without needing to be physically present at the factory."

Every product and design decision should be checked against this sentence. If a feature doesn't make shopping easier for customers **or** make the business more visible to its owner, question why it's being built.

## Core goals

1. Move the business off manual/paper record-keeping onto one digital system.
2. Give the owner real-time visibility ("a heartbeat") into the business without being physically present.
3. Increase sales and make purchasing easier for customers.
4. Give business partners/investors full transparency into their investment performance.
5. Reduce wastage and loss through better tracking and calculation.

## What this platform explicitly is NOT (for now)

- Not a marketplace connecting unrelated third-party sellers.
- Not an affiliate/referral/commission platform — "partners" means business investors, not marketing affiliates.
- Not a multi-factory system (single factory for the foreseeable future).
- Not migrating any historical manual records — starting fresh.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning in this project |
|---|---|
| Partner / Investor | A person who has invested money in the business and receives quarterly profit share. Not an affiliate. |
| MOQ | Minimum Order Quantity for wholesale eligibility — confirmed as **20 units**. |
| Batch | A production run of a given product/style, tracked through manufacturing stages. |
| Custom/Pre-order | A buyer-submitted design request, quoted manually, requiring sample approval before full production. |
| Central system | The single backend all sales channels (online, in-store, wholesale) must feed into — no channel operates in isolation. |
